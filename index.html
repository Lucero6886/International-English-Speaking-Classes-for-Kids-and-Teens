🎮 My Favorite Hobbies & Free Time

🎯 Today's Speaking Objectives:

  • Use vocabulary related to hobbies and free-time activities.
  • Answer personal questions about likes and dislikes fluently.
  • Express and explain opinions about different activities.

📖 2. Vocabulary

Click on a word card to learn its meaning and see an example sentence.

Indoor (adj)
Happening inside a building.
"Reading is a great indoor hobby."
Outdoor (adj)
Happening outside in the fresh air.
"Hiking and cycling are popular outdoor activities."
Collect (verb)
To get and keep things of one type.
"My brother likes to collect old coins."
Creative (adj)
Producing or using original and unusual ideas.
"Painting is a very creative hobby."
Relax (verb)
To rest and become calm.
"I listen to music to relax after school."
Instrument (noun)
An object used for making music.
"She plays a musical instrument, the piano."
Join (verb)
To become a member of an organization or club.
"I want to join the photography club."
Practice (verb)
To do something regularly to improve your skill.
"You must practice playing the guitar every day."

🗣️ 3. Main Speaking Practice

Answer the questions below. If you need help, click the buttons for ideas or sample answers!

Easy 1. What is your favorite hobby?
Idea Prompts:
• Name a sport (football, swimming).
• Name an art (drawing, playing piano).
• Name a technology activity (video games, coding).
Short Answer:
"My favorite hobby is playing basketball."
Extended Answer:
"My favorite hobby is playing basketball. I love it because it is exciting and fast. I usually play with my friends at the park."
Easy 2. When do you usually do your hobby?
Idea Prompts:
• After school / On weekends.
• Every evening / In the morning.
• When I finish my homework.
Short Answer:
"I usually do my hobby on the weekends."
Extended Answer:
"I usually do my hobby on the weekends, specifically on Saturday afternoons. During the week, I am too busy with homework."
Easy 3. Who do you do your hobby with?
Idea Prompts:
• By myself (alone).
• With my best friend / siblings.
• With my club or team.
Short Answer:
"I usually practice my hobby with my sister."
Extended Answer:
"I prefer to do my hobby alone. When I draw or paint, I need a quiet space to focus and be creative."
Medium 4. Why do you enjoy this activity?
Idea Prompts:
• It helps me relax / reduces stress.
• It makes me healthy and strong.
• I can learn new skills and be creative.
Short Answer:
"I enjoy it because it helps me relax after a long day at school."
Extended Answer:
"I really enjoy this activity because it helps me reduce stress. Whenever I feel tired from studying, playing music makes me feel calm and happy."
Medium 5. Is your hobby expensive? Does it cost a lot of money?
Idea Prompts:
• No, it is free (like running or reading).
• Yes, I have to buy equipment (like a camera or guitar).
• Only a little bit (like buying paper and pencils).
Short Answer:
"No, my hobby is very cheap. I just need a pencil and paper."
Extended Answer:
"Yes, photography can be quite expensive. You have to buy a good camera, and sometimes you need to buy different lenses and a tripod."
Medium 6. Do you prefer indoor or outdoor hobbies? Why?
Idea Prompts:
• Outdoor: Like fresh air, nature, moving around.
• Indoor: Like staying comfortable, no rain, quiet space.
Short Answer:
"I prefer outdoor hobbies because I like breathing fresh air."
Extended Answer:
"I definitely prefer indoor hobbies. I don't like getting hot or sweaty outside. I would rather stay in my comfortable room and read a good book or play games."
Medium 7. What is a popular hobby for teenagers in your country?
Idea Prompts:
• Playing mobile games (Esports).
• Watching TikTok or YouTube.
• Hanging out at cafes / Playing football.
Short Answer:
"In my country, playing mobile games is very popular for teenagers."
Extended Answer:
"In my country, many teenagers love playing mobile games with their friends online. Also, watching and making short videos on TikTok is a huge hobby right now."
Opinion 8. Do you think playing video games is a good hobby?
Idea Prompts:
• Yes: Improves reaction time, good way to socialize online.
• No: Bad for your eyes, makes you lazy, waste of time.
Short Answer:
"Yes, I think it is good because it helps you relax."
Extended Answer:
"I think playing video games is a good hobby if you don't play too much. It can improve your reaction time and logic. However, playing for 5 hours a day is bad for your health."
Opinion 9. Is it important to learn new hobbies? Why?
Idea Prompts:
• Yes, it keeps your brain active.
• Yes, you can meet new people.
• Yes, it helps you discover your talents.
Short Answer:
"Yes, it is important because you can learn new skills."
Extended Answer:
"In my opinion, learning new hobbies is very important. It keeps your brain active and prevents you from getting bored. Also, trying new things helps you discover hidden talents."
Opinion 10. Should parents choose hobbies for their children?
Idea Prompts:
• No, children should choose what they enjoy.
• Yes, but only when they are very young.
• Parents should suggest, but not force.
Short Answer:
"No, I think children should choose their own hobbies."
Extended Answer:
"I don't think parents should choose hobbies for teenagers. If parents force a child to play piano, the child might hate it. Children should choose activities they truly enjoy."

🎭 4. Role-Play Scenarios

Work with a partner. Choose a scenario and create a conversation. Click the hints if you need help.

Scenario A: Joining a Club

Situation: Student A wants to join the school Photography Club. Student B is the club leader.

Role A: Ask questions about the club (when it meets, what you need).

Role B: Welcome Student A and answer their questions.

A: "Hi, I want to join the club. When do you meet?"
B: "We meet every Friday after school."
A: "Do I need a big camera?"
B: "No, your phone camera is fine!"

Scenario B: The Weekend Plan

Situation: Student A wants to try a new outdoor hobby this weekend. Student B prefers indoor hobbies.

Role A: Invite B to go hiking or cycling. Say why it's good.

Role B: Politely say no. Suggest an indoor activity instead.

A: "Let's go cycling this Saturday! The weather is great."
B: "I'm sorry, I don't really like cycling. It makes me tired."
A: "Oh, okay. What do you want to do?"
B: "Why don't we stay inside and play video games?"
