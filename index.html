🎮 Hobbies & Tech 📱

Let's talk about what we love doing in our free time!

🎯 Today's Goals:

  • ✅ Talk about hobbies and how often you do them.
  • ✅ Describe favorite tech gadgets and why you use them.
  • ✅ Express simple opinions about screen time.

1️⃣ Warm-up

🔥

Let's get talking! Answer these questions with your teacher or partner.

Q1: What is your favorite thing to do on a Saturday? 🛋️

"On Saturdays, I love sleeping in and then playing football in the park with my friends."

Q2: Do you prefer playing outside or staying inside? 🌳/🏠

"I prefer staying inside because I love playing video games and reading comics."

Q3: How many screens do you have in your bedroom? 💻

"I have two screens: my smartphone and my laptop for doing my homework."

Q4: What is the most popular hobby in your country? ⚽

"In my country, playing soccer and going to cafes are very popular hobbies."

Q5: If you could invent a new gadget, what would it do? 🤖

"I would invent a robot that does my homework and cleans my room perfectly in 5 minutes!"

4️⃣ Role-play Scenarios

🎭

Work with your partner. Choose a role and act out the situation!

Scenario A: The New Hobby

Situation: You want to start a new after-school club (like Coding or Drama) but you don't want to go alone. Try to convince your friend to join you.

Student A: The one who wants to join the club. Give reasons why it will be fun.
Student B: The friend. You are not sure. You prefer playing video games at home. Ask questions.
"Hey, do you want to join the... with me?"

"I don't know, I usually..."

"But it will be fun because..."

"How much time will it take?"

Scenario B: Screen Time Rules

Situation: You want to play a video game for one more hour, but you already used your daily screen time. Talk to your parent.

Student A: The teenager. Ask politely and promise to do chores (housework) later.
Student B: The Parent. You are strict. Say no at first, but maybe make a deal.
"Mom/Dad, can I please have one more hour on..."

"You know the rules. You already spent..."

"I promise I will clean my room if..."

"Okay, but only if you..."
