Future Tech:
My Dream Gadget

Age: 10-14 Level: A1-B1 60 Mins

Learning Objectives

  • Learn technology and invention vocabulary.
  • Describe everyday problems and tech solutions.
  • Express opinions about the future fluently.

1. Warm-Up

1. What is your favorite tech device right now? 📱

Example: "My favorite device is my tablet because I can play games and watch videos on a big screen."

2. If you had a robot, what would it do for you? 🤖

Example: "If I had a robot, it would clean my room and do my math homework!"

3. Which is better: playing outside or playing video games? 🌳🎮

Example: "I think playing outside is better because I can run and get fresh air with my friends."

4. Do you want to travel to space one day? 🚀

Example: "Yes! I want to travel to space to see the stars and float without gravity."

5. What is something you hate doing every day? (Maybe a new gadget can fix it!) 😩

Example: "I hate waking up early. I need a bed that gently wakes me up with music and hot breakfast!"

4. Role-Play Scenarios

Scenario 1: The Tech Store

You are buying a birthday gift for your best friend. You are at an electronics store looking for a cool gadget.

Student A: Customer

  • Say what you are looking for (gift for friend).
  • Ask about battery life and price.
  • "Can you recommend a good gadget?"

Student B: Shop Assistant

  • Greet the customer and ask to help.
  • Suggest a smart watch or wireless headphones.
  • "How about this one? It has a very long battery."

Scenario 2: Shark Tank Pitch

You invented a crazy new machine (e.g., an automatic homework-doer). Try to convince an investor to give you money!

Student A: Inventor

  • Introduce your gadget's name and what it does.
  • Explain why everyone needs it.
  • "My invention is the Homework-Bot 3000. It is useful because..."

Student B: Investor

  • Listen and ask difficult questions.
  • Ask about the price and if it is safe.
  • "That sounds cool, but is it safe for children? How much does it cost?"
