Scenario A

Starting a School Club

Situation: Your school wants to open a new after-school club.

Task: Talk together and choose the best club: Art Club, Sports Club, or Coding Club.

A
Student A
  • Goal: You really want the Sports Club.
  • Mention: Students need to be active and healthy.
B
Student B
  • Goal: You prefer the Art Club or Coding Club.
  • Mention: Sports can be too tiring. We need to learn skills.

A: "I think the Sports Club is best because..."

B: "I see your point, but coding is very useful for the future."

Scenario B

Weekend Plans

Situation: You and your friend have a free weekend.

Task: Decide what to do together: Go to the cinema, play video games, or go cycling.

A
The Gamer
  • Goal: You want to stay home and play games.
  • Mention: It's comfortable and cheap.
B
The Active Friend
  • Goal: You want to go outside (cinema or cycling).
  • Mention: We play too many games during the week.

A: "Why don't we just stay home and play Minecraft?"

B: "Let's do something active! How about we go cycling in the park?"
